The problem

It is not a training problem. It is a capability problem.

Training gets scheduled because it is always scheduled, not because anyone has confirmed it closes a real gap. Without a clear picture of what people can already do, it is guesswork dressed up as a plan.

A capability view separates what is actually missing from what simply has not been checked, so effort goes where it changes outcomes, not just where it is easiest to run a course.

How we look at capability

Capability has three parts.

Mindset

How they think and behave.

Skillset

What they know and can do.

Toolset

What machines, systems and technology they can use.

Mindset + Skillset + Toolset = Manufacturing capability

How we assess

Four ways to see the full picture.

Self assessment

People reflect on what they can do today, in their own words.

Manager assessment

A manager or supervisor view of demonstrated performance on the job.

Practical observation

Watching the work happen and checking it against a defined standard.

Knowledge check

A short check of the understanding behind the skill, not just the action.
